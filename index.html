The process of my design...



Approach to Design

Understanding what the client wants and what the viewer wants, then bringing those perspectives together through design.

Prototyping

Building interactive prototypes in Figma so a design can actually be clicked through, from arrow-key menus to tappable app screens.

Users

Understanding who is using the design and what they need from it so the experience actually works for them.



Featured Work



This is your Destiny

This Is Your Destiny is a Game Boy style game and I designed its menus in Figma in a green pixel palette. The title screen, continue screen, options, and pause menu are all prototyped so you can move through them with the arrow keys.

See the project



Halo&Haze App Design

Halo&Haze is a made up Asian and Latin cafe and I designed its ordering app in Figma which is inspired by Dutch Bros. The app is made to have a warm and modern look with sage green as the main color! The screens cover ordering, a loyalty and rewards section, and a profile page with saved items and past orders. The tagline is “Stay in the haze a little longer.”

See the project



NoriBox Website

NoriBox is a game console brand and I designed its website in Figma. The pages cover the home screen, the NoriPass membership, the NoriBox 1 through 4 devices.

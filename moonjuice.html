The Concept

Moonjuice is a made up lip serum product focused on hydration and soothing care. I wanted to explore a beauty product that felt approachable & memorable.

Product & Packaging

The project explores the product's packaging and how it could be presented to potential customers. I created the social media post and used a mockup to visualize the serum tube and its application.

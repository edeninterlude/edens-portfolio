Zombie OC



Keep running, keep shooting. Try not to die.


The Character

An original character created for an endless side scrolling zombie shooter concept. He's a tired looking guy with messy black hair, an olive-green sweater, and a personality that doesn't exactly match the seriousness of the apocalypse!

His personality: sarcastic, laid-back, humorous, somehow still getting himself into trouble.

The Game Concept

The game concept follows the character as he runs through an endless side scrolling setting. He fights off waves of zombies and tries to survive for as long as possible.

The goal was to combine simple gameplay with a character who brings some personality and humor to an otherwise chaotic setting.
